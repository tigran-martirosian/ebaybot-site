Privacy policy

ebaybot photos asks for one Google permission: access to the files it creates in the signed-in account's Google Drive (the drive.file scope). It cannot see any other file.

It uses that access only to upload product photos and rename the folders it made. Nothing is collected, stored elsewhere, sold or shared. The sign-in token stays on the owner's computer.

To remove its access: Google Account, Security, Third-party apps, remove "ebaybot photos".
